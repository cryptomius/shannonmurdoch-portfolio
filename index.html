    The loop

    I own the whole feedback loop, not just the middle of it.

    Insight in, design in the middle, measurement out – plus the commercial and technical constraints that decide what’s actually shippable. Tools change; the loop doesn’t.

    01 / COLLECT

    Customer insight

    Recruit, script and moderate. Unmoderated remote studies at volume, plus session replay to catch the confusion nobody reports.

    UserTestingMazePostHog replaySprigSupport tickets
    02 / SYNTHESISE

    Reference & reuse

    Findings tagged and searchable so the next project starts from evidence instead of the last argument. Design principles drawn from research become the trade-off criteria.

    DovetailNotionConfluenceJourney mapsJTBD
    03 / SYSTEMATISE

    Design systems

    Tokens, variables, component APIs and the governance around them. I’ve founded one from a blank page and maintained one in code.

    Figma variablesDesign tokensStorybookshadcn/uiTailwindRadix
    04 / BUILD

    Mockups & prototypes

    High-fidelity when the question is visual, clickable when the question is flow, coded when the question is feasibility. AI generation where it saves time, never where it costs judgement.

    FigmaPolymet.aiClaude CodeNext.jsTypeScript
    05 / SHIP

    Engineering partnership

    Dev-ready handoff, sign-off boundaries, and standing rituals so developers shape the work before it’s built rather than rebuilding it after.

    LinearJiraGitHubDesign reviewSpec automation
    06 / MEASURE

    Impact & lifecycle

    Event instrumentation designed alongside the interface, so drop-off by step is available the day it ships — then feeds the next prioritisation round.

    PostHogGA4BigQueryTableauA/B testingCustomer.io
