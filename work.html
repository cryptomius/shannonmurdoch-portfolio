Case studies

Four projects, written up honestly.

Including the rounds where testing told me my design was wrong, and the two occasions I had to reverse out of a direction I’d already sold internally.

Everything at Webjet was designed under my UX leadership between 2015 and 2021 and remains commercially sensitive only where noted. Screens shown are the original artefacts from the projects.

01 Webjet UX research Interaction design Conversion

Unblocking a purchase-path dead-end

Customers who picked a paired international fare landed on a screen where every returning flight appeared unavailable. A problem the business had circled for two years with three unbuilt solutions on the table. Six prototype rounds later, people understood it.

+0.2% conversion on Hybrid Matrix, +0.4% on International Matrix — about +$500k AUD TTV per month

02 Webjet Mobile 6 test rounds API constraints

Rebuilding flight search for mobile

Inherited external agency wireframes the single-call flight API could not support, plus a legacy mobile site and iOS app that had drifted apart. Six rounds of remote usability testing to a flow people finished without help — two of those rounds proved my fix made things worse.

Eliminated both confusion points that had survived three rounds of iteration

03 Webjet Design systems Stakeholder strategy Org change

Founding Webjet's first UI Pattern Library

Two brands, four countries, seven products, fifteen years of accumulated UI drift, no design standards, and back-end developers implementing front-end work. Getting a pattern library funded was really an argument about resourcing, ownership and workflow — not about buttons.

A dedicated front-end team funded; developers reaching for the library by default within six months

Also shipped

Work that’s live but not written up.

Happy to walk through any of these in a conversation.

Flight price heatmap

Webjet · 2017–2019

Surfacing cached flight prices per date across routes with wildly varying search volume — technically the hardest UX enhancement I championed there. I designed the four-colour algorithm that decided what counted as cheap or expensive on a given route, and prototyped and usability-tested it before build.

Every purchase & post-purchase interface

Webjet · 2015–2021

Flights, hotels, packages and car hire, across responsive web and native iOS/Android. Search widgets, results, cart and ancillaries, payment, success states, itinerary management — plus the email and PDF templates across the booking lifecycle, because printed artefacts still matter to travellers.

Design ops rescue

Eclipse Fi · 2023–2025

Designs were being thrown over the fence from a cluttered Figma canvas and developers were guessing which version was approved. I introduced a signed-off / in-progress workflow with per-product indexes, plus twice-weekly UX–front-end stand-ups so developer input shaped work before it was built. Rebuilds dropped; morale recovered.

Live on-chain dashboards

Eclipse Fi & LVT Capital · 2020–2025

A token public-sale fundraising dashboard reading directly from on-chain RPCs, customer retention Sankey flows across raise events, and a market-data aggregation and visualisation system used to surface investment opportunities.
